How the Peruvian marketing industry is out-strategizing everyone else right now

Instagram carousel, @buttereightco

Context

In my time collaborating with Peruvian small businesses it was evident their processes were fine-tuned differently from what I was seeing in American marketing.

Caption

Cultivated from a huge influx of homegrown entrepreneurs comes an incredibly potent wave of aestheticians, café owners, nail techs, and workout studios. And with them, a just as hungry class of creatives.

They were forged out of an economic plague that has seemingly affected the last four generations, in a country that has felt left behind by its economy and government for decades. Tuition for higher education may not have been on the table, and neither were the formal professional pipelines that come from networking coffee chats and LinkedIn notes. Home-based businesses had to grow from grit. From that, self-taught photographers, videographers, tastemakers, and concept creators have emerged as this era’s strongest marketers.

Working alongside them are Peru’s new grad marketers, fresh out of school and already holding their own next to people who learned it all on the job. Together, they’re behind most of the incredible marketing coming out of the country right now.
